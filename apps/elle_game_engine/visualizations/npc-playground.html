🎯 How to Use the Playground

Real-Time Interaction

Type messages to the NPC and watch their emotional state evolve based on the conversation. The 3D emotion sphere visualizes their current emotional state in the PAD (Pleasure-Arousal-Dominance) model.

Manual Emotion Control

Use the sliders to manually adjust the NPC's emotional state and see how it affects their personality:

  • Valence: Pleasant (+1) ↔ Unpleasant (-1)
  • Arousal: Calm (0) → Excited (1)
  • Dominance: Submissive (0) → Dominant (1)
  • Trust: Distrustful (0) → Trusting (1)

Demo Mode vs Live LLM

In Demo Mode, responses are simulated based on keyword matching. Connect to a real LLM provider for genuine AI-powered conversations that adapt to context and emotion.

Export & Analysis

Export your conversation as JSON to analyze how emotions evolved over time or to import into the BigPlay engine for further processing.
