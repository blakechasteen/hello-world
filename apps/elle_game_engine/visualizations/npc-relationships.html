🎯 How NPC Relationships Work

Dynamic Relationship System

BigPlay tracks NPC relationships that evolve based on player actions and world events:

Relationship Attributes

  • Type: FRIEND, ENEMY, ROMANCE, FAMILY, RIVAL, MENTOR, BUSINESS
  • Strength: 0.0 (weak) to 1.0 (strong) - affects dialogue and quest availability
  • History: Event timeline (first met, major interactions, betrayals, etc.)
  • Visibility: PUBLIC (everyone knows) or SECRET (hidden from others)

Relationship Changes

# Initial state: Alice (Merchant) and Bob (Guard) are friendly
{"type": "FRIEND", "strength": 0.7}

# Player steals from Alice's shop, caught by Bob
# Bob's loyalty to Alice increases
{"type": "FRIEND", "strength": 0.85}

# Player convinces Bob to let them go
# Bob feels conflicted, relationship weakens
{"type": "FRIEND", "strength": 0.6}

# Later, Alice discovers Bob let the thief escape
# Relationship degrades to rivalry
{"type": "RIVAL", "strength": 0.4}

Transitive Relationships

NPCs infer relationships through their network:

  • "Friend of a friend": Positive first impression (trust +0.2)
  • "Enemy of my enemy": Potential alliance opportunity
  • "Family of enemy": Inherited hostility (unless proven otherwise)

Graph Queries

# Find all NPCs who might help based on relationships
MATCH (player)-[:FRIEND]-(ally)-[:FRIEND]-(potential_help)
WHERE potential_help.location = 'castle'
RETURN potential_help, ally.introduction_willingness

# Detect love triangles
MATCH (a)-[:ROMANCE]-(b)-[:ROMANCE]-(c)-[:ROMANCE]-(a)
RETURN a, b, c

# Find most influential NPC (highest degree centrality)
MATCH (npc)-[r]-()
RETURN npc.name, count(r) as connections
ORDER BY connections DESC
LIMIT 1
