================================================================================
TUFTE PRINCIPLES APPLIED
================================================================================

1. Maximize data-ink ratio (~60-70% vs ~30% traditional)
2. Show data variation, not design variation
3. Reveal data at several levels (overview → detail)
4. Small multiples for comparison
5. Sparklines for inline trends
6. Minimal decoration, maximum information
7. Consistent scales for fair comparison
8. Word-sized graphics (sparklines)
