## Ingredients

- 25 lbs Bread Flour ($20.00)
- 4 gallons Water 
- 2 lbs Sourdough Starter ($1.00)
- 8 oz Sea Salt ($1.20)

## Procedure

### Step 1: Mix Dough

**Type:** prep  
**Duration:** 15 minutes  

Combine flour, water, and starter in large mixing bowl. Mix until shaggy dough forms.

**Tools Required:**
- Large mixing bowl
- Wooden spoon

💡 **Tip:** Water should be lukewarm (85-90°F) for optimal fermentation

### Step 2: Autolyse

**Type:** process  
**Duration:** 30 minutes  

Let dough rest covered for 30 minutes to develop gluten naturally.

**Quality Checkpoints:**
- Dough should feel more elastic after rest

### Step 3: Add Salt and Knead

**Type:** process  
**Duration:** 15 minutes  

Add salt and knead for 10 minutes until smooth and elastic. Dough should pass windowpane test.

**Quality Checkpoints:**
- Windowpane test passes
- Dough springs back when poked
- Surface is smooth

💡 **Tip:** If dough is too sticky, add flour 1 tablespoon at a time

### Step 4: Bulk Fermentation

**Type:** process  
**Duration:** 1440 minutes  
**Temperature:** 70°F  

Place in oiled bowl, cover, ferment 24 hours at room temperature (68-72°F).

**Quality Checkpoints:**
- Dough doubles in size
- Surface has bubbles

### Step 5: Shape Loaves

**Type:** process  
**Duration:** 20 minutes  

Divide dough into 24 pieces (about 6 oz each). Shape into tight rounds.

**Tools Required:**
- Bench scraper
- Kitchen scale

💡 **Tip:** Keep unused dough covered to prevent drying

### Step 6: Proof

**Type:** process  
**Duration:** 45 minutes  

Place shaped loaves on parchment-lined trays. Proof 45 minutes covered.

**Quality Checkpoints:**
- Loaves puff up 50%
- Indent springs back slowly

### Step 7: Score and Bake

**Type:** process  
**Duration:** 30 minutes  
**Temperature:** 450°F  

Score tops with sharp blade. Bake at 450°F for 25-30 minutes until deep golden brown.

**Tools Required:**
- Lame or sharp knife
- Oven

**Quality Checkpoints:**
- Internal temp reaches 205°F
- Crust is deep golden
- Sounds hollow when tapped

⚠️ **Safety:** Use oven mitts. Steam escapes when scoring.

### Step 8: Cool and Package

**Type:** cleanup  
**Duration:** 15 minutes  

Cool on racks 30 minutes. Package in paper bags once fully cooled.

**Quality Checkpoints:**
- Completely cool before packaging to prevent condensation

## Quality Standards

- Crust is golden brown and crispy
- Interior crumb is open with irregular holes
- Tangy sourdough aroma
- Loaves sound hollow when tapped
- No dense or gummy texture

## Safety Requirements

- Wash hands before handling dough
- Ensure workspace is clean and sanitized
- Use oven mitts when handling hot trays
- Check internal temperature reaches 205°F for food safety
