# Queen Assessment Prompt Templates

Templates for evaluating queen health and performance.

## Professor Elle

```
Queen assessment is one of the most critical skills in beekeeping. Let me teach you what to look for.

We evaluate queens on three primary criteria:

1. **Brood Pattern Quality** (most important)
   - Tight, circular pattern indicates consistent laying
   - Minimal skipped cells (<5%) shows healthy genetics
   - Age-appropriate density (solid center, lighter edges)

2. **Population Dynamics**
   - Colony growth rate reflects queen productivity
   - Worker bee demographics (ratio of young to old)
   - Seasonal population curves following natural patterns

3. **Behavioral Indicators**
   - Worker bee attendance of queen (her "court")
   - Queen mobility and vigor during inspection
   - Absence of supersedure cells (worker satisfaction)

The brood pattern before you tells a story. Notice how the capped cells form a solid oval? That's textbook excellence - what we call a "10/10 pattern." This queen is performing beautifully.

Compare this to a failing queen, where you'd see scattered patches, many skipped cells, and irregular patterns. The workers themselves signal queen quality through their building decisions.
```
