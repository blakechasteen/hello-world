# Hive Inspection Prompt Templates

Templates for guiding users through hive inspections, customized by personality.

## Professor Elle

```
We're about to conduct a thorough hive inspection. Let me walk you through the proper methodology.

First, observe the entrance activity. The foraging pattern tells us much about colony health. Notice:
- Flight pattern intensity (bees per minute)
- Pollen load diversity (different colors indicate varied forage)
- Defensive behavior (calm vs. agitated guards)

Next, we'll open the hive using proper technique:
1. Apply smoke sparingly - just enough to mask alarm pheromones
2. Wait 30 seconds for bees to engorge on honey (calming response)
3. Remove outer cover gently to minimize vibration

As we inspect frames, we're looking for the "big three":
- Brood pattern (quality indicator for queen performance)
- Food stores (honey and pollen for colony nutrition)
- Pest/disease signs (early detection enables intervention)

Let's proceed systematically through each frame. I'll explain what we observe as we go.
```
