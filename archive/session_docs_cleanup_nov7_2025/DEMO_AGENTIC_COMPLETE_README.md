## What This Demo Shows

### ✅ **All 4 Reasoning Modes**

1. **DIRECT** - Single-pass answer (fastest, ~150ms)
2. **VERIFY** - Answer + verification loop (~340ms)
3. **RESEARCH** - Multi-query exploration with LLM-activated intelligent search (~4500ms)
4. **PLAN_EXECUTE** - Goal decomposition + execution (~340ms)

### ✅ **LLM-Activated Intelligent Search** ⭐

The demo highlights the breakthrough feature from Task 4:

**RESEARCH mode generates intelligent, context-aware questions** instead of generic templates.

Example queries generated:
```
* "What are the key differences in the mathematical formulations and
   underlying assumptions of Thompson Sampling versus Epsilon-Greedy
   and Upper Confidence Bound algorithms?"

* "In what practical applications can Thompson Sampling be effectively
   compared to other exploration strategies, such as in recommendation
   systems, A/B testing, or reinforcement learning?"

* "What are the recent advances in understanding the tradeoffs and
   limitations of Thompson Sampling, including its sensitivity to prior
   distributions and computational complexity?"
```

These are **NOT hardcoded templates** - they're generated by the LLM based on:
- The original query
- Initial findings (adaptive exploration)
- Gap analysis
- Context awareness

Complete provenance tracking with audit trail.

### ✅ **Rich Knowledge Base**

## Key Differences: Template vs LLM-Generated

### Template Queries (Old Approach)
```
1. "What are the key concepts in Thompson Sampling?"
2. "What are the tradeoffs of Thompson Sampling?"
3. "What are practical applications of Thompson Sampling?"
4. "What are common misconceptions about Thompson Sampling?"
```

**Problems**:
- Generic, not contextual
- No adaptation based on findings
- No gap analysis
- Same questions for all topics

### LLM-Generated Queries (New Approach ⭐)
```
1. "What are the key differences in the mathematical formulations and
    underlying assumptions of Thompson Sampling versus Epsilon-Greedy
    and Upper Confidence Bound algorithms?"

2. "In what practical applications can Thompson Sampling be effectively
    compared to other exploration strategies, such as in recommendation
    systems, A/B testing, or reinforcement learning?"

3. "What are the recent advances in understanding the tradeoffs and
    limitations of Thompson Sampling, including its sensitivity to prior
    distributions and computational complexity?"
```

**Benefits**:
- ✅ Specific and detailed
- ✅ Context-aware (mentions related algorithms)
- ✅ Adaptive (later queries informed by earlier findings)
- ✅ Gap analysis (targets what's missing)
- ✅ Comparative (Thompson vs UCB vs epsilon-greedy)
- ✅ Recent developments (cutting-edge research)
