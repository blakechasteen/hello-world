## Example (Mini Version)

**Problem:** Calculate the compound interest on $1000 at 5% for 2 years

### Step 1: Understand
- What is being asked? Final amount after 2 years with compound interest
- Key terms: Compound interest (interest on interest)
- Given: Principal $1000, rate 5%, time 2 years
- Missing: Compounding frequency (assume annual)

### Step 2: Decompose
- Subproblem 1: Calculate first year interest
- Subproblem 2: Calculate second year interest (on new principal)
- Subproblem 3: Sum to get final amount

### Step 3: Analyze
- Subproblem 1: $1000 × 0.05 = $50 → New principal $1050
- Subproblem 2: $1050 × 0.05 = $52.50 → New principal $1102.50
- Total interest: $50 + $52.50 = $102.50

### Step 4: Synthesize
- Pattern: Each year's interest is calculated on growing principal
- Formula: A = P(1 + r)^t = 1000(1.05)^2 = $1102.50

### Step 5: Validate
- ✓ Answers the question
- ✓ All subproblems solved
- ✓ Makes sense (more than simple interest)
- ✓ Can explain: money grows faster because interest earns interest

### Step 6: Conclusion
**Final Answer:** $1102.50
**Confidence:** High (straightforward calculation)
**Summary:** Compound interest adds $102.50 vs $100 simple interest
