## SOP: Bake Bread Loaf

**Category:** Bakery
**Estimated Time:** 2.0 hours
**Estimated Cost:** $2.50 (materials) + $50.00 (labor @ $25/hr) = $52.50
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Output:** 1 bread loaf
**Created:** 2025-11-15
**Last Updated:** 2025-11-20

### Steps:
1. Mix dry ingredients (flour, salt, yeast) - 15 min
2. Add wet ingredients (water, oil), knead dough - 20 min
3. First rise (covered, room temp) - 60 min
4. Shape loaf, place in pan - 10 min
5. Second rise (covered) - 30 min
6. Bake at 350°F - 25 min
7. Cool on rack - 10 min

### Materials:
- Flour: 500g ($1.50)
- Water: 350ml (free)
- Yeast: 10g ($0.50)
- Salt: 10g ($0.10)
- Olive oil: 30ml ($0.40)

### Equipment:
- Mixing bowl
- Measuring cups/spoons
- Bread pan
- Oven

### Notes:
- Use room temperature water (80-90°F)
- Cover during rises with damp towel
- Loaf should sound hollow when tapped
- Can freeze for up to 3 months

### Common Issues:
- **Dense loaf:** Not enough kneading or rise time
- **Flat loaf:** Too much water or weak yeast
- **Burnt crust:** Oven too hot, reduce to 325°F

## SOP: Prepare Biochar Batch

**Category:** Production
**Estimated Time:** 4.0 hours
**Estimated Cost:** $15.00 (materials) + $100.00 (labor @ $25/hr) = $115.00
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Output:** 10 biochar kits (1 lb each)
**Created:** 2025-10-15
**Last Updated:** 2025-11-18

### Steps:
1. Collect raw biomass (wood chips, branches) - 30 min
2. Prepare pyrolysis barrel - 15 min
3. Load biomass into barrel - 20 min
4. Start controlled burn - 45 min
5. Monitor pyrolysis process - 120 min
6. Cool down (water quench) - 30 min
7. Break up large pieces - 20 min
8. Package into 1 lb bags - 20 min

### Materials:
- Biomass (wood chips): 15 lbs ($5.00)
- Pyrolysis barrel: amortized ($2.00)
- Packaging bags (10): $3.00
- Labels (10): $1.00
- Safety equipment: amortized ($4.00)

### Equipment:
- Pyrolysis barrel (55-gallon drum)
- Heat source (propane torch)
- Safety gear (gloves, goggles, mask)
- Water source for quenching
- Hammer for breaking chunks

### Notes:
- Work outdoors only (smoke)
- Keep fire extinguisher nearby
- Proper ventilation required
- Biochar ready when all smoke stops

### Safety Warnings:
- **Fire hazard:** Keep clear of flammable materials
- **Smoke inhalation:** Wear respiratory protection
- **Burns:** Hot barrel stays hot for hours

## SOP: Package Cookies Batch

**Category:** Bakery
**Estimated Time:** 1.5 hours
**Estimated Cost:** $3.50 (materials) + $37.50 (labor @ $25/hr) = $41.00
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Output:** 24 individually packaged cookies
**Created:** 2025-11-05
**Last Updated:** 2025-11-19

### Steps:
1. Prepare dough (mix ingredients) - 20 min
2. Portion dough into cookies - 15 min
3. Bake in batches (12 per batch) - 30 min total
4. Cool cookies completely - 20 min
5. Wrap individually in cellophane - 20 min
6. Apply labels (flavor, date) - 10 min
7. Pack into display box - 5 min

### Materials:
- Flour: 400g ($1.00)
- Sugar: 200g ($0.40)
- Butter: 200g ($1.20)
- Eggs: 2 ($0.40)
- Chocolate chips: 150g ($1.00)
- Cellophane wrap: 24 ($1.20)
- Labels: 24 ($0.60)
- Display box: 1 ($0.70)

### Equipment:
- Mixing bowl
- Cookie scoop (standardize size)
- Baking sheets (2)
- Cooling racks
- Heat sealer (for cellophane)

### Notes:
- Use cookie scoop for consistent size (2 tbsp)
- Space cookies 2 inches apart on sheet
- Bake at 350°F for 12-14 minutes
- Cookies should be golden brown at edges

### Quality Standards:
- Uniform size (2 inches diameter)
- Golden brown (not burnt)
- Completely cooled before wrapping
- Sealed cellophane (no air leaks)
- Labels clear and straight
