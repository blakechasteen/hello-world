## 💰 COST-SAVING STRATEGIES

### General Money-Saving Tips

1. **Buy Used When Possible**
   - Facebook Marketplace: Mason jars, equipment, shelving (save 50-70%)
   - Thrift Stores: Baking pans, mixing bowls, kitchen tools (save 60-80%)
   - Craigslist: Restaurant equipment, fermenters (save 40-60%)

2. **Bulk Buying**
   - Costco membership pays for itself with flour alone
   - Split bulk orders with friends (seeds, jars, supplies)
   - Buy seasonal items on sale (canning supplies in Sept)

3. **DIY Alternatives**
   - Make labels at home (free templates online)
   - Use recycled containers for seed starting
   - Build cold frames from old windows + scrap wood

4. **Strategic Timing**
   - Black Friday / Cyber Monday: Kitchen equipment (30-50% off)
   - End of season: Garden supplies (40-60% off)
   - Post-Christmas: Baking supplies clearance (25-40% off)

5. **Cashback & Rewards**
   - Amazon Prime Visa: 5% back on Amazon purchases
   - Costco Anywhere Visa: 2% back at Costco
   - Rakuten cashback: 2-10% back at many retailers

### Product-Specific Savings

**Flour** (Biggest Ongoing Cost):
- **Costco**: $0.36/lb (best value)
- **Azure Standard**: $0.32/lb (co-op buying, bulk delivery)
- **Direct from mill**: $0.40-50/lb (better quality, support local)
- **Don't buy**: Grocery store 5lb bags ($0.90-1.20/lb - too expensive)

**Jars & Containers**:
- **Facebook Marketplace**: $0.25-50/jar (vs $1.00+ new)
- **Yard Sales**: Often free if buying other items
- **Ask local restaurants**: May give away pickle jars, etc.

**Seeds**:
- **Save your own seeds**: Free after first year
- **Seed swaps**: Free seeds from local gardeners
- **Buy bulk**: $50-100 worth lasts multiple years

**Costco**
- Best for: Flour, yeast, bulk ingredients, potting soil

## 💡 FINAL SHOPPING TIPS

1. **Always Compare Prices**
   - Use browser extensions: Honey, Capital One Shopping (auto-find coupons)
   - Check 3 sources before buying (especially expensive items)
   - Factor in shipping costs (free shipping may not be cheapest)

2. **Buy Quality Where It Matters**
   - Good scale: Accuracy matters for recipes (spend $20, not $10)
   - Good knife: Daily use, safety issue (spend $30, not $10)
   - Good fermenters: Avoid contamination (spend $30, not $15)

3. **Skimp on Aesthetics Initially**
   - Pretty labels can wait (function first)
   - Matching containers not essential (use what you have)
   - Fancy displays not needed (focus on product quality)

4. **Buy Ahead on Sales**
   - Mason jars: Buy in August-Sept (50% off)
   - Baking equipment: Buy Black Friday (30-50% off)
   - Garden supplies: Buy in fall (clearance 40-60% off)

5. **Track Your Spending**
   - Keep all receipts
   - Track in spreadsheet or app
   - Review weekly (stay on budget)
   - Calculate ROI per item (was it worth it?)

6. **Ask for Help**
   - Friends/family may have equipment to borrow/give
   - Local Buy Nothing groups (free items)
   - Bartering (trade bread for equipment/supplies)
