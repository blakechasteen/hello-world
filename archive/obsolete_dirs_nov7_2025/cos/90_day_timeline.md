## ⚠️ RISK FACTORS & CONTINGENCIES

### Production Risks

**RISK**: Bread quality inconsistent
**CONTINGENCY**: Document exact recipes, invest in better flour, get training

**RISK**: Meal prep doesn't sell
**CONTINGENCY**: Pivot to different items, adjust pricing, improve marketing

**RISK**: Micro brewing batch fails
**CONTINGENCY**: Have backup batches, insurance against losses, learn from failure

### Financial Risks

**RISK**: Revenue below projections
**CONTINGENCY**: Cut expenses, delay Phase 2/3 expansion, focus on best sellers

**RISK**: Unexpected expenses (equipment failure)
**CONTINGENCY**: Maintain 10-20% cash reserve, have backup equipment plans

**RISK**: Seasonal demand fluctuations
**CONTINGENCY**: Diversify product mix, plan for seasonality, build reserves

### Operational Risks

**RISK**: Burnout (working too many hours)
**CONTINGENCY**:
- Track time religiously
- Take 1 day off per week
- Reduce product lines if >50 hrs/week
- Hire help if revenue supports it

**RISK**: Space constraints
**CONTINGENCY**: Complete clearing by Week 8, rent additional space if needed

**RISK**: Supply chain issues
**CONTINGENCY**: Maintain 2-week ingredient buffer, have backup suppliers
