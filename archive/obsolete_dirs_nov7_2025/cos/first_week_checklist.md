## 🚨 RISK MITIGATION

### If Bread Doesn't Rise Properly
- **Backup Plan**: Yeast may be issue. Buy new yeast immediately.
- **Pivot**: Focus on honey/seed sales while troubleshooting.

### If Equipment Doesn't Arrive
- **Backup Plan**: Use existing kitchen tools, handwrite labels.
- **Communication**: Update customers on timing.

### If No Sales Materialize
- **Backup Plan**: Give away first loaves to friends/family for feedback.
- **Adjust**: Lower prices, improve marketing, change venues.

### If Space Clearing Stalls
- **Backup Plan**: Work in smaller functional areas.
- **Delegate**: Ask for help if overwhelmed.

### If Burnout Sets In
- **Backup Plan**: Take Day 4 or 5 as partial rest day.
- **Adjust**: Reduce production targets, focus on quality over quantity.
