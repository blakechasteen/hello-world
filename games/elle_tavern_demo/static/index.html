How to Play

Welcome to The Rusty Mug!

This is an interactive text adventure powered by Elle Game Engine.

Controls:

  • Talk to NPCs: Click on an NPC, then type your message
  • Quick Responses: Use the dropdown for common phrases
  • Voice: Toggle voice synthesis on/off
  • Look Around: Get a fresh description of your location
  • Save/Load: Save your progress and load previous games

NPC Moods:

NPCs have emotions that change based on your interactions:

  • 😊 Happy - Positive and friendly
  • 😠 Angry - Upset or hostile
  • 😟 Sad - Melancholy or worried
  • 😲 Surprised - Excited or shocked
  • 😐 Neutral - Calm and balanced

Tips:

  • Be polite to NPCs to build trust
  • Ask about quests to find adventures
  • Explore dialogue options - NPCs remember your conversations
  • Save often!
