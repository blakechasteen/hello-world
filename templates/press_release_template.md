## Proactive Disclosure Press Release

```
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

HoloLoom Announces Data Security Incident; Takes Swift Action to Protect Customers

Company Detects and Contains Unauthorized Access; Offers Free Credit Monitoring

[City, State] – November 16, 2025 – HoloLoom, Inc., a leading provider of neural
decision-making systems, announced today that it has detected and successfully
contained a data security incident affecting approximately 15,000 customer accounts.

The company detected unauthorized access to its customer database on November 16, 2025.
Upon immediate investigation, HoloLoom's security team determined that an attacker
gained access through a phishing email and exploited a vulnerability in the company's
website to download customer information.

"We take the security and privacy of our customers' data very seriously," said
[CEO Name], CEO of HoloLoom. "Upon detection, we immediately launched our incident
response protocols, contained the attack, and are providing our customers with the
information and resources they need to stay safe."

INCIDENT DETAILS
─────────────────

When: The attack occurred on November 16, 2025, beginning at approximately 2:15 UTC
and was detected at 14:30 UTC the same day.

What: The attacker accessed customer database containing the following information:
  • Customer names
  • Customer email addresses
  • Customer phone numbers
  • Hashed passwords (not plaintext, significantly reducing fraud risk)

How Many: Approximately 15,000 of HoloLoom's 200,000 customers were affected, representing
approximately 7.5% of the customer base.

What We Didn't Lose: The attacker did NOT access:
  • Payment card information (HoloLoom does not store this data)
  • Social Security Numbers (HoloLoom does not collect this information)
  • Government ID information
  • Health or medical information

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS TAKEN
────────────────────────

Upon detection, HoloLoom immediately:

1. Contained the Attack
   • Blocked the attacker's IP address
   • Revoked the compromised employee credentials
   • Patched the vulnerability that was exploited
   • Secured all affected systems

2. Launched Investigation
   • Engaged leading cybersecurity forensics firm (Mandiant)
   • Notified law enforcement
   • Preserved all forensic evidence
   • Determined root cause and scope

3. Notified Customers
   • Customer notifications began November 17, 2025
   • Clear information about what data was affected
   • Specific steps customers should take
   • Offered 24 months of free credit monitoring

4. Implemented Safeguards
   • Implemented multi-factor authentication (MFA) for all admin accounts
   • Enhanced email security to prevent similar phishing attacks
   • Deployed database monitoring to detect future unauthorized access
   • Enhanced logging and alerting

CUSTOMER SUPPORT & PROTECTION
──────────────────────────────

HoloLoom is offering affected customers:

• Free Credit Monitoring: 24 months of complimentary credit monitoring and
  identity protection services (valued at $250+)

• Support Team: 24/7 incident support hotline (1-800-HOLOLOOM ext. SECURITY)
  and email support for customer questions

• Transparency: Complete timeline of the incident and FAQ on company website

• Identity Protection: Guidance on credit freezes and fraud protection

"We recognize that a security incident can be concerning for our customers," said
[CSO/Chief Information Security Officer Name], Chief Information Security Officer
of HoloLoom. "We're committed to supporting our customers through this process and
ensuring they have the tools and information needed to protect themselves."

ROOT CAUSE & PREVENTION
────────────────────────

The investigation determined that the attack was made possible through two factors:

1. A phishing email that convinced one of our employees to provide their password
2. A SQL injection vulnerability in our web application login functionality

HoloLoom is taking comprehensive steps to prevent similar incidents:

Immediate (Within 30 days):
  ✓ Mandatory multi-factor authentication (MFA) for all administrative accounts
  ✓ Enhanced email security with advanced threat protection
  ✓ Patched SQL injection vulnerability
  ✓ Enhanced employee security training and phishing simulations

Short-term (Within 90 days):
  ✓ Deploy data loss prevention (DLP) system
  ✓ Implement database activity monitoring
  ✓ Conduct security audit of all user-controllable input points
  ✓ Increase security awareness training across organization

Long-term:
  ✓ Pursue ISO 27001 certification
  ✓ Conduct quarterly security assessments
  ✓ Implement zero-trust architecture
  ✓ Establish annual tabletop incident response exercises
```
