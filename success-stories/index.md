## Common Questions

**Q: How much time will I save?**
A: Depends on the workflow. On average: 8-12 hours/week. Individual workflows range from 1-15 hours/week saved.

**Q: How much does it cost?**
A: $10-50/month for HoloLoom subscription. Individual workflows cost $0.01-0.10 per execution.

**Q: How long to set up?**
A: Pre-built workflows: <5 minutes. Custom workflows: 1-2 hours.

**Q: Will it work for my use case?**
A: We have 100+ templates. Likely yes. Browse [Workflow Gallery →](../workflow-gallery.md)

**Q: What if I need something custom?**
A: Use the visual workflow builder (no code) or write custom code.

**Q: Is my data private?**
A: Yes. Workflows run on your infrastructure (local, cloud, or our servers - you choose).
