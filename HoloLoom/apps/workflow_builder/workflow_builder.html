💡 Pro Tips:
• Press D on a selected node to quickly duplicate it
• Use Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V to copy nodes across workflows
• Press T to quickly access workflow templates
• Use undo/redo (Ctrl+Z/Ctrl+Y) to experiment safely
• Press H to open action history and click to restore any past state
• Press P to view performance metrics, latency trends, and Thompson Sampling priors
• Press L to auto-arrange nodes with hierarchical, force-directed, or radial layouts
• Press G to group selected nodes (or create an empty group)
